Electrical Engineering Formulas Reference

1. Voltage Drop ($\Delta V$)

Single Phase: $\Delta V = \frac{2 \cdot L \cdot I \cdot (R\cos\phi + X\sin\phi)}{1000}$

Three Phase: $\Delta V = \frac{\sqrt{3} \cdot L \cdot I \cdot (R\cos\phi + X\sin\phi)}{1000}$

2. Power Factor Correction

Active Power: $P = S \cdot \cos(\phi_1)$

Required kVAR: $Q_c = P \cdot [\tan(\arccos(PF_1)) - \tan(\arccos(PF_2))]$

Capacitance: $C = \frac{Q_c}{2\pi \cdot f \cdot V^2}$

3. Passive Filter Cutoff Frequency

RC Cutoff: $f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi R C}$

RL Cutoff: $f_c = \frac{R}{2\pi L}$
